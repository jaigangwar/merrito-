RBMI Admission Hub CRM

Project Completion & Integration Guide

Overview

The new RBMI Admission Hub CRM is 100% developed and technically ready for production. All 29 frontend modules, automation workflows, and backend systems have been successfully completed, tested, and secured.

This document outlines the final administrative details needed from the RBMI management to connect the CRM to the real world and take it live.

What is Completed & Working?

  • Lead Management Pipeline: The system architecture is built and ready to automatically capture leads (once API credentials are provided) with a fully working drag-and-drop pipeline.
  • Smart Lead Distribution: Automatic assignment of leads to counselors based on predefined rules (Round Robin, Load Balancing).
  • Communication Suite: Email, SMS, and WhatsApp integrations are technically integrated.
  • Asha AI Assistant: Floating AI chatbot for both counselors and students.
  • Applicant Portal: Complete student portal for document uploads and application tracking.
  • Form Builder: Drag-and-drop tool to create custom admission forms that can be embedded on any website.
  • Payment Gateway Integration: Full Razorpay payment desk for fee collection.

Information Needed from College Administration

To take the project live, we need to replace the demo data with real college credentials. Please provide the following details:

1. Official Communication Channels

  • Admissions Email Address: (e.g., admission@rbmi.edu.in) & the SMTP App Password.
  • Helpline Number: Official admission contact number to display in automated SMS & WhatsApp messages.
  • Twilio/MSG91 Credentials: For sending actual SMS and WhatsApp messages to students.

2. Payment & Finance

  • Razorpay Credentials: Key ID and Key Secret from the college's official Razorpay merchant account to process online fee payments.
  • Account Name: Official name on the bank account.

3. Lead Generation Sources

Please provide login access or Webhook URLs for the active lead generation platforms to automate the data inflow:

  • Shiksha.com
  • CollegeDekho
  • JustDial
  • Facebook Ads Manager (Lead forms)

4. Team & Academic Details

  • List of Counselors: Names, phone numbers, and official email IDs of all admission counselors.
  • Final Course List: A finalized list of all active courses with their respective tuition fees for the current academic session.

5. Domain & Hosting Setup

  • Subdomain Creation: We need DNS access or request the IT team to point crm.rbmi.edu.in to our hosting server.
  • Hosting Account: Access to a cloud hosting provider (Render.com or Railway.app) to deploy the database and backend servers.

Final Steps to Go Live

  1. Collect the 5 categories of information listed above.
  2. Enter the official credentials into the CRM's secure environment.
  3. Deploy the application to the cloud server.
  4. Go Live!
